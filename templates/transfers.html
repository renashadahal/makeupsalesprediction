Transfer Workflow Policy

Strict 3-step physical custody lifecycle ensures zero phantom stock and complete ledger auditability across branch nodes.

1. Request Phase (PENDING) Destination initiates request. Source branch inventory is checked for availability.
2. Dispatch Phase (IN_TRANSIT) Source branch operator (or admin) approves & dispatches; source inventory is debited immediately.
3. Confirmation Phase (COMPLETED) Destination branch operator (or admin) confirms physical arrival; destination inventory is credited.
Admin Override: Administrators have override authority to approve dispatch, confirm receipt, or cancel transfers at any step.
